## Review rules applied

- Case endings, tanwīn, article/conjunction shells and attached pronouns were reviewed separately and were not counted as new lexical Ṣarf members unless they teach a genuine number, person or possession contrast.
- Bare roots, provider lemmas without review, duplicate occurrences and root-only relations were rejected.
- Model-issue and blocked decisions remain non-publishable until the required expert/model evidence is available.
