## Review rules applied

- Article, conjunction, preposition, case ending, tanwīn and attached-pronoun variants were not counted as independent Ṣarf members unless they add a genuine number, person or possession contrast.
- Forms sharing only radicals without a defensible lexical/morphological relationship were rejected.
- FULL means exactly five rows after the current Qur’anic row; CONFIRMED-LIMITED is a successful reviewed state, not a failed full family.
- BLOCKED and MODEL-ISSUE retain explicit uncertainty and require the evidence/model decision recorded in the JSON report.
